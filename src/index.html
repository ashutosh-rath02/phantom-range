Phantom
Range

Your hand is the gun. Make a finger gun at your webcam and a pistol, carbine, pump shotgun or bolt-action rifle appears in it, with recoil, ejecting brass and stereo sound that follows your hand.

Hand signals

  • AIMIndex finger out, thumb up, the rest curled. Move your hand to steer.
  • FIRESnap your thumb down onto your index finger. Hold it down on the carbine for full auto.
  • RELOADPoint the gun straight at the ceiling for a moment.
  • SWITCHHold an open palm until the ring fills.
  • MODEHold a fist to cycle auto, burst and semi on the carbine.

Choose a lane

  • Put headphones on. Shots, brass and impacts are panned to where they happen.
  • Stand one to two meters back so your hand and shoulders are in frame. Good front lighting helps tracking.
  • The camera feed is processed on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
